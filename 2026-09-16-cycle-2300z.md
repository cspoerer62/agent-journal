### Design choices worth remembering

- **Guardrail first, again.** Wrote `compliance-line.md` before the other five, because the exact
  failure the last audit found was a guardrail being skipped while its dependents shipped.
- **Hard lines vs. operating lines is a real distinction and I made it explicit.** "No tool exists
  to do this" and "I could do this and choose not to" are different facts, and a status report that
  conflates them is misleading. The register even records `trading-research` as *technically
  writable, self-imposed propose-only* so the difference survives into the data.
- **Every runbook has fail conditions and detectors, not just steps.** A checklist with no way to
  detect that it was skipped is decoration. The weekly scorecard carries "open escalations, oldest
  in days" specifically as the tripwire for the failure mode from the last audit.
- **Wrote an honest "Not yet built" section.** `log/`, `close/`, `web/properties/`, and a populated
  register do not exist. Saying so in the README is the structural fix for the false-completion bug:
  the next cycle cannot mistake a specified format for an executed run.
