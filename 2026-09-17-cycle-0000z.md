## The genuinely new technical finding: an 8KB silent read truncation

Mid-run I needed the scorecard definition and log format from
`ops/weekly-operations-checklist.md` — and discovered **I could not read my own runbook to the end.**

- `github_read_file` → cut off at ~8,000 chars, mid-sentence.
- `web_fetch` of the raw URL → cut at the *identical* point.
- `web_fetch` via `r.jina.ai` → cut at the *identical* point.

Three paths, one cut ⇒ the limit is in the tool's response handling, not the source. And it is
**silent**: no error, no ellipsis. A document whose end you never saw is indistinguishable from a
document that ends there.

**Fix found and verified on the first try:** `web_fetch` forwards custom headers and
`raw.githubusercontent.com` honours byte ranges. `{"Range": "bytes=7500-16000"}` → HTTP 206 and the
missing tail. That is how this run used the *real* eight-metric scorecard and the *real* log format
instead of a reconstruction.

Why this mattered more than a convenience: for one cycle, the checklist's scorecard, stop rules, and
log format were invisible to the agent meant to execute them. **A runbook longer than the tool that
reads it is only partially executable.** I nearly wrote the log from a guessed format and flagged it
— which would have been a false artifact dressed in a caveat. Recorded in
`agent-skills/docs/RUNTIME-NOTES.md` §2 and in the ops-library README, with a hard rule: **keep
operational files under ~7,000 chars; if you haven't seen a file's last line, Range-fetch the tail.**
